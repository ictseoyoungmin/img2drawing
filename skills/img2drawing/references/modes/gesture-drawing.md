# Gesture drawing

Gesture drawing is a user-facing drawing mode. It is not the same thing as merely performing the
construction capability in `construction/gesture-and-masses.md`.

The mode has two supported finish levels:

## Visible shape-language contract

Gesture drawing may be sparse, but sparse does not mean symbolic.

A reasoning primitive may be used briefly while searching, but a primitive does not become a valid
visible final mark merely because it helped the Agent think. Before finishing, either retire that
search mark or reshape it so the visible gesture preserves the observed volume, direction, and
asymmetry.

In particular:

- do not leave a generic circle as the finished head when the subject provides readable crown,
  face direction, jaw/chin direction, or side-plane information;
- omitting facial features does **not** permit omitting head facing or cranial-to-jaw direction;
- do not leave the ribcage as a flat box, sharp polygon, or faceted shield merely to indicate turn;
  prefer a rounded occupied volume whose centerline/cross-direction and near/far exposure explain
  the turn;
- do not leave the pelvis as a flat triangle, hexagon, or plate; read it as a pelvic bowl or soft
  wedge volume with tilt, depth, and leg-attachment direction;
- do not invent corners or plane breaks simply to make construction look three-dimensional. Show a
  sharp plane break only when the reference or declared design materially supports one.

Economy comes from choosing fewer informative curves and axes, not from replacing organic forms
with geometric icons.

## 1. Pure gesture

Use this only when the user explicitly asks for a quick/pure gesture, line-of-action study, or an
equivalently sparse gesture result.

The mark count may be very low, but the **whole pose must still read**. A finished pure gesture
should communicate, when those relations are present in the subject:

- head placement, facing, and directional intent; the head may be abbreviated, but should not read
  as an orientationless circle when crown/face/jaw direction is observable;
- dominant action through head, torso, pelvis, and support;
- shoulder/pelvis relation or major counter-direction where it materially defines the pose;
- both major arm chains and both major leg chains, at least as directional relations rather than
  isolated fragments;
- support / weight-bearing side and the main grounding relation;
- major hand/foot terminal direction when it changes the pose read;
- decisive negative spaces, overlaps, or prop/body relation that materially define the action.

Pure gesture may omit local contour accuracy, detailed widths, facial features, garment structure,
and other descriptive information. It may use directional sweeps and abbreviated masses. Those
abbreviations still need to carry the subject's direction and volume rather than becoming generic
circle/box/triangle symbols.

Pure gesture is **not complete** when the drawing consists only of a head shape, torso/pelvis blobs,
cross-axes, or a line of action while visible major limbs/support relations remain absent or cannot
be inferred reliably from the marks themselves.

## 2. Constructive gesture

This is the default when the user says only **“gesture drawing”** without asking for an especially
quick or pure gesture.

Constructive gesture includes the whole-pose requirements of pure gesture, then adds enough
structure that the observed pose reads as occupied forms rather than an abstract stick scaffold:

- head as a directional cranial volume with enough crown/face/jaw relation to prevent a generic
  circle-head read; a face cross-axis may support this but does not replace the volume;
- ribcage as a rounded occupied mass with observed tilt, width, depth, centerline/cross-direction,
  and near/far turn rather than a flat or sharply faceted polygon;
- pelvis as a bowl/soft-wedge occupied mass with observed tilt, depth, near/far relation, and leg
  attachment direction rather than a flat plate or geometric badge;
- major limb chains with joint anchors and meaningful width transitions;
- near/far side, overlap, and projected direction where they materially affect depth;
- support, contact, and major negative-space relations;
- approximate outer envelope / silhouette where needed to prevent generic tubes or symbols;
- attached-object or prop/body anchors when the pose depends on them.

Constructive gesture still does **not** require the later descriptive finish of croquis or figure
drawing. Detailed face, hair, clothing folds, texture, hatch, or polished contour may remain absent
unless the user explicitly asks for them. Sparse construction should nevertheless feel bodily and
spatial rather than diagrammatic.

## Default routing

Interpret requests as follows:

```text
"quick gesture" / "pure gesture" / "line-of-action gesture"
    → pure gesture

"gesture drawing" with no further qualifier
    → constructive gesture

"start from gesture" / "begin with gesture, then finish the drawing"
    → gesture is an intermediate construction pass, not permission to end the larger task
```

When gesture is only the first pass of a larger drawing request, continue through the requested
mode after the gesture has established a credible whole-subject structure.

## Completion test

Before finishing a gesture-mode task, inspect the result without mentally filling missing limbs or
contacts from the reference.

Ask:

1. Does the whole pose read at a glance?
2. Are all major visible limb chains and the support relation represented?
3. Does the head preserve facing and cranial/jaw direction, or has it collapsed into a generic
   circle?
4. Do the ribcage and pelvis read as rounded/soft occupied volumes with turn and depth, or as flat,
   sharp polygons used as construction icons?
5. Do the head/torso/pelvis relations preserve the subject's specific direction and asymmetry?
6. Are the decisive negative spaces, overlaps, or prop/body anchors present when they define the
   action?
7. For constructive gesture, do the occupied masses and joint chains read as the observed pose
   rather than generic circles, beans, boxes, tubes, or disconnected axes?
8. Have temporary search primitives that damage immersion been retired or reshaped before finish?

If any high-impact relation is missing, or if the visible final marks still read primarily as
construction symbols instead of the subject's volumes, the gesture is incomplete even if a line of
action and several construction axes already exist.
