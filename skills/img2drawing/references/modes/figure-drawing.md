# Figure drawing mode

## Purpose

Observe longer than in croquis mode to resolve connected anatomy and form,
shoulder-elbow-wrist and hip-knee-ankle chains, overlap and contact, hands and feet,
garment-to-body structure, and the relational identity required by the request.

## Suggested grammar

```text
gesture/envelope → connected volume masses → joint and limb continuity
→ garment/body relation → overlap/contact → hands/feet → selective identity finish
```

## Completion questions

Do the landmarks and body chains agree across the whole pose? Are overlap, contact,
hands, feet, clothing hang, and the face/hair relationship structurally legible?
Do not invent a terminal hidden by a pocket, garment, prop, or another form. Neither the
mode name nor stroke count determines completion.
