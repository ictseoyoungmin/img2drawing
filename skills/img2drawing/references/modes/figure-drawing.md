# Figure drawing

Figure drawing keeps the whole-body relationship primary while allowing more resolved form
than croquis.

Start from action, occupied masses, balance, and limb chains. Then resolve contour,
foreshortening, anatomy/garment ownership, face/hair identity, hands, feet, clothing, and
attached objects according to visible importance.

Construction may remain lightly visible when it contributes rhythm or form, but descriptive
lines must eventually own the final silhouette and important internal boundaries. Do not
use anatomical convention to overwrite clothing, occlusion, perspective, or the actual
subject.
