# Tonal study mode

## Purpose

Explain form and space through light/shadow families, the largest value shapes, form
turns, and hard/soft/lost edge hierarchy rather than the quantity of lines.

## Suggested grammar

```text
correct form → declared light → large shadow family → authored fill_region decisions
→ reserved light → form turn → edge hierarchy → selective dark accents
```

Group the large shadow family first; do not fragment form with local values or arbitrary
bands. Record each broad tone as one authored region decision and let deterministic
contacts expand only during render/replay. Machine-authored microhatching must not become
hundreds of artistic actions. Let construction lines recede when needed, but do not allow
the renderer or a filter to invent light, form, edges, or geometry.

## Completion questions

Do light and shadow families read before local detail? Does each value region sit inside
a structurally correct form? Do edge changes explain light, material, or spatial turn?
A tonal study is not a separate pipeline that repeats croquis stages.
