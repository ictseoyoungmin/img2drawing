# Optional P6 Identity Finish — relational features and selective line expression

P6 is opt-in. Use it only when the request needs a recognizable face, hair grouping,
clothing identity or equipment signature beyond the P5 under-drawing. The default
`full_body_croquis` registry ends at P5; the optional
`full_body_croquis_with_p6` registry adds this stage without changing the earlier
contracts.

## Preflight

Before drawing, inspect the P1–P5 whole-view and high-risk crops. If head direction,
torso turn, occupied volume, garment connection, joint continuity or contour ownership
is unresolved, reopen the earliest responsible stage. Detail cannot repair macro structure.
The runtime records this decision in `identity/preflight.json` and refuses a P6 manifest
while an upstream decision is `revise` or a region is a blocker.

## Feature and hair order

1. Re-state the eye line and face centreline from the locked observation.
2. Place both eyes, nose, mouth and chin as a proportional interval; near/far asymmetry
   follows the head turn. Do not place arbitrary eyes on an enclosing circle.
3. Draw the outer hair mass seated on the cranium, then a few grouped locks and tips that
   explain the face opening. Leave the skull/face wedge visible.
4. Add only identity-defining garment breaks and folds at an anchor, tension or
   compression event. Do not fill the surface with wrinkles or stitching.
5. Use selective form/accent restatement on the high-information 15–25% of the drawing.

## Material contract

`CalibrationSheet` records five pressure samples, curves and taper values in the actual
canvas coordinate system. Translate construction/form/accent hierarchy to the current
pencil `grade`, `pressure`, `width`, `opacity` and explicit per-point pressure. Values
from another drawing engine are not portable. Every P6 action remains replayable.

The default profile limits identity marks to 48, confirmation marks to 12, micro-folds to
8, and accents to at most 25% of identity marks. These are guardrails against blanket
darkening, not an artistic score.

## Closure

Keep P5 and P6 as separate deliverables. A fresh whole-view and applicable crop review
must state the face relation, grouped hair, sparse garment structure and line hierarchy.
Mechanical manifest validity proves only provenance and budget compliance; the evaluator
still owns the visual decision.
