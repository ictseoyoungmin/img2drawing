# P1 gesture lines

P1 carries **three different lines**, and merging any two of them breaks the drawing:

1. **Facial centreline** — crown → nose → chin, curved across the head's surface. It
   explains where the *face* is turned. A plain vertical centre line carries no rotation.
2. **Spine centreline** — an S-curve starting *behind the neck* (the cervical C-curve),
   through mid-back, waist and sacrum. It explains the *body's* gesture. It never starts
   at the chin.
3. **Line of action** — the whole figure's energy. It typically enters above the head,
   cuts diagonally across the body, and lands ahead of the weight-bearing foot. It is not
   the spine.

Draw them as separate strokes; their visual rhythm should still flow into one another.

Attached objects may appear as a restrained major axis when they change the envelope, but
must not overpower the body gesture.
