# Joints across the pipeline
P2 states **where** the joints are: shoulder/elbow/wrist and hip/knee/ankle as positions,
with limb segments as straight or lightly cylindrical axes, so the
`hip → knee → ankle → foot` chain is explicit.

P3 gives those segments **volume** and taper.

P4 explains how the real form — sleeve, trouser, footwear — hangs across the chain.

Full guidance: `../stages/p2-primary-axes.md`, `../stages/p4-structural-connections.md`.
