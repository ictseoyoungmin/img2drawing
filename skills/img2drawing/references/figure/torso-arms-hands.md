# Torso, arms, and hands

Keep the torso and arms as connected spatial relationships rather than one merged clothing
shape or separate limb symbols.

Read ribcage/pelvis orientation, shoulder insertion, armpit/side-plane transitions, elbow
location, forearm taper, wrist direction, and hand contact. Clothing may own the visible
contour; do not force an anatomical edge where fabric or occlusion is what is actually
seen.

## Arm-chain ownership

Hands are terminals of an arm chain. Establish shoulder → elbow → wrist before treating a
hand mismatch as local. If reach, wrist entry, or negative space is wrong, repair the parent
chain in `../construction/balance-and-limbs.md` instead of redrawing the hand repeatedly.

When a segment is strongly compressed in image-space or points toward/away from the viewer,
use `../construction/foreshortening-and-depth.md` before normalizing it to an expected
anatomical length.

## Local hand handoff

Once the parent chain is credible, route visible palm/finger grouping, thumb opposition,
mitten-like simplification, pocket insertion, grip, and detailed hand/object contact to
`hands-and-grip.md`.

Do not draw knuckles or fingers that are hidden. Use overlap and negative space to separate
arm from torso when similar values make the boundary ambiguous.
