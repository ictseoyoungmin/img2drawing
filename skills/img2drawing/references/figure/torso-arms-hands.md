# Torso, arms, and hands

Keep the torso and arms as connected spatial relationships rather than one merged clothing
shape or separate limb symbols.

Read ribcage/pelvis orientation, shoulder insertion, armpit/side-plane transitions, elbow
location, forearm taper, wrist direction, and hand contact. Clothing may own the visible
contour; do not force an anatomical edge where fabric or occlusion is what is actually
seen.

## Torso ownership and turn

A torso is not closed merely because one outer garment silhouette exists. Preserve the visible
relations that explain ribcage/pelvis turn, shoulder/back/side exposure, taper, waist/hem relation,
and near/far asymmetry where the reference supports them.

If both sides become generic parallel/rounded boundaries and the torso could fit many unrelated
subjects, reopen orientation/width-depth structure before adding seams, pockets, or hatch marks.

## Arm-chain ownership

Hands are terminals of an arm chain. Establish shoulder → elbow → wrist before treating a
hand mismatch as local. If reach, wrist entry, or negative space is wrong, repair the parent
chain in `../construction/balance-and-limbs.md` instead of redrawing the hand repeatedly.

A sleeve or arm is not resolved by two clean side lines alone. Where visible, preserve at least one
instance-specific relation such as shoulder insertion, near/far edge asymmetry, elbow break,
forearm taper, compression, twist, overlap, or wrist orientation. If the arm still reads as a tube,
reopen it rather than decorating the tube with folds.

When a segment is strongly compressed in image-space or points toward/away from the viewer,
use `../construction/foreshortening-and-depth.md` before normalizing it to an expected
anatomical length. In close perspective, verify that depth propagation continues through the
entire shoulder→elbow→wrist chain rather than stopping at an enlarged head/shoulder mass.

## Local hand handoff

Once the parent chain is credible, route visible palm/finger grouping, thumb opposition,
mitten-like simplification, pocket insertion, grip, and detailed hand/object contact to
`hands-and-grip.md`.

Do not draw knuckles or fingers that are hidden. Use overlap and negative space to separate
arm from torso when similar values make the boundary ambiguous.

Before finish, use `../review/visual-quality-gates.md` to check tube/rail shorthand, ownership
handoffs, hierarchy, and construction retirement.
