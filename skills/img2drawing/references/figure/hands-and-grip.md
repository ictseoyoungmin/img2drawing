# Hands and grip

Use this leaf when the parent shoulder → elbow → wrist chain is already credible and the
remaining mismatch belongs to the visible hand, grip, or contact itself.

A hand is not a mitten, fan, or five-finger symbol. Croquis may use very few marks, but those
marks must preserve the observed hand orientation, grouping, overlap, and contact.

## Observe the hand as a terminal form

Read:

- wrist axis and the hand's change of direction from the forearm;
- palm/back-of-hand envelope and visible plane turn;
- thumb origin, opposition, and whether it crosses, presses, or disappears behind another form;
- visible finger groups, gaps, bends, and terminations;
- which edge belongs to hand, garment, pocket, body, or prop;
- compression or opening created by the actual contact.

Do not begin by counting fingers. First establish the palm/hand mass and the few visible
relationships that make the gesture specific.

## Author with grouped geometry

Prefer a small number of informative contours and overlap lines. Separate fingers only where
a visible gap, bend, silhouette break, or contact requires separation. A grouped pair of
fingers can be one shape when the reference supports it; a clearly separated fingertip must
not be absorbed into a generic mitten merely to save strokes.

Do not invent knuckles, fingertips, or hidden digits to make an occluded hand feel complete.
Stop each form where the evidence stops.

## Grip and object contact

A believable grip is a shared relation between hand and object, not independent drawings that
happen to overlap.

Preserve:

- the object's axis and thickness through the contact;
- where the palm or finger group actually bears against it;
- thumb opposition when visible;
- foreground/background ownership at each handoff;
- wrist orientation required by the visible contact.

Do not bend the hand arbitrarily to rescue a wrongly placed prop, and do not move the prop to
rescue a wrong arm chain. Route the responsible premise upstream instead.

## Escalate when local hand drawing is not the cause

- shoulder/elbow/wrist chain or reach is wrong → `../construction/balance-and-limbs.md`;
- projected depth or severe compression is wrong → `../construction/foreshortening-and-depth.md`;
- prop axis/topology/contact anchor is wrong → `../props/attached-objects.md`;
- contact ownership or occlusion is ambiguous → `../description/contour-and-overlap.md`;
- visible evidence is still uncertain → `../observation/visual-observation.md`.

Repeatedly adding finger lines to an unchanged mitten-like silhouette is not refinement. Re-observe
and replace the terminal geometry or escalate to the relation that actually caused it.
