# Dual-reference review
Subject and exemplar answer different questions.

- Subject: Where is the body? What carries weight? What overlaps what?
- Exemplar: What information belongs in this stage? How economical are the marks?
- Drawing: What did the Agent actually commit?

A stage can match the subject yet still be a poor P1/P2/P3 representation; it can also resemble an exemplar while copying the wrong pose. Review both separately.
