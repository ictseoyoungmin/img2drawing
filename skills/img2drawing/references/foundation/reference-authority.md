# Reference authority

Reference authority defines what the drawing is allowed to treat as truth.

## Observed

A readable subject is the sole geometry authority. Crops, overlays, edge views, grids,
measurements, and material samples are derived evidence only. They may clarify the subject
but may not overrule visible geometry.

Do not copy coordinates, forms, or stylistic solutions from unrelated drawings. Do not
infer a hidden hand, foot, jaw, garment edge, or prop ending merely because such a part is
usually present.

### Visible projection outranks normalization

Category knowledge, anatomical plausibility, remembered character designs, model sheets, and
other priors may help detect uncertainty. They are **diagnostic priors, not correction authority**.

If the readable reference shows a relation that looks unusual, stylized, distorted, asymmetric,
foreshortened, or anatomically surprising, do not silently replace it with the relation you expect.
Instead:

1. re-observe the visible relation at useful crop/whole scales;
2. check neighboring anchors, overlap, negative space, and projected orientation;
3. distinguish genuine ambiguity from a merely unfamiliar projection;
4. if the visible evidence remains coherent, preserve that evidence in the drawing.

This applies especially to hand chirality/grouping, compressed limbs, face proportions, hair shape,
clothing design, prop geometry, and strong-perspective terminals. A visually odd reference is not
permission to unfold, symmetrize, beautify, anatomically repair, or canonically redesign it.

For a named or familiar subject, the **particular supplied reference instance** outranks memory of
how that subject usually looks. Do not replace observed hairstyle, facial structure, costume,
accessory, pose, or silhouette with a remembered canonical version unless the user explicitly asks
for that transformation.

If some higher-priority constraint prevents faithful preservation of a visible relation, do not
silently substitute a different identity, anatomy, style, or pose and present it as faithful.
Surface the limitation or route the task appropriately instead.

## Imaginative

When no subject exists, the declared composition, shape, relationship, and style goals are
the authority. Evidence is drawing-only. Never fabricate a reference image and then claim
it is observed evidence.

## Hybrid

Keep preserved constraints distinct from transformed constraints. A requested
transformation does not grant permission to drift unrelated pose, proportion, contact, or
composition unless those relationships were also declared transformable.

## Precedence

When instructions conflict:

`reference/declaration geometry → structural relationships → requested finish → style`

A style preference never justifies knowingly changing the subject's pose, silhouette,
identity, or contact relationships. Category priors and remembered canonical designs never outrank
a readable observed relation.
