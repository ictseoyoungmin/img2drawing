# Reference authority

Reference authority defines what the drawing is allowed to treat as truth.

## Observed

A readable subject is the sole geometry authority. Crops, overlays, edge views, grids,
measurements, and material samples are derived evidence only. They may clarify the subject
but may not overrule visible geometry.

Do not copy coordinates, forms, or stylistic solutions from unrelated drawings. Do not
infer a hidden hand, foot, jaw, garment edge, or prop ending merely because such a part is
usually present.

## Imaginative

When no subject exists, the declared composition, shape, relationship, and style goals are
the authority. Evidence is drawing-only. Never fabricate a reference image and then claim
it is observed evidence.

## Hybrid

Keep preserved constraints distinct from transformed constraints. A requested
transformation does not grant permission to drift unrelated pose, proportion, contact, or
composition unless those relationships were also declared transformable.

## Precedence

When instructions conflict:

`reference/declaration geometry → structural relationships → requested finish → style`

A style preference never justifies knowingly changing the subject's pose, silhouette,
identity, or contact relationships.
