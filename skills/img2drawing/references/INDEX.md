# Instruction graph index

This file is a **map**, not a textbook, lifecycle, or preload curriculum.
Start at `SKILL.md`, then open only the smallest leaves that own the current problem.

Paths below are relative to the `references/` root. `SKILL.md` is one level above and therefore
uses the `references/...` prefix when it links to the same leaves.

## Foundation

- `foundation/reference-authority.md`
  - open when: deciding what visual/declarative source is allowed to define the drawing, or when plausibility conflicts with the supplied reference
  - owns: observed/imaginative/hybrid authority and anti-normalization
- `foundation/line-economy.md`
  - open when: deciding which marks are necessary or when repeated/search lines are accumulating
  - owns: economical semantic grouping without geometry loss
- `foundation/structural-specificity.md`
  - open when: construction is becoming generic or a descriptive pass is about to inherit earlier structure
  - owns: structural specificity and revalidation of provisional construction
- `foundation/occlusion-inference.md`
  - open when: a materially important relation disappears behind another form
  - owns: minimum hidden continuity reasoning versus visible rendered appearance
- `foundation/scope-and-precedence.md`
  - open when: multiple requirements or correction priorities compete
  - owns: scope, precedence, and macro-before-micro priority

## Drawing modes

Choose the smallest mode leaf that matches the requested finish target.

- `modes/gesture-drawing.md`
  - open when: the requested mode is gesture drawing or gesture is an explicit intermediate pass
  - owns: pure versus constructive gesture and gesture-specific completion
- `modes/croquis.md`
  - open when: the requested finish is croquis
  - owns: croquis-specific mark economy and finish boundary
- `modes/figure-drawing.md`
  - open when: the requested finish is a more resolved figure drawing
  - owns: figure-drawing emphasis and finish expectations
- `modes/line-study.md`
  - open when: the requested finish emphasizes line rather than tonal rendering
  - owns: line-study finish semantics
- `modes/tonal-study.md`
  - open when: the requested finish materially depends on value/light
  - owns: tonal-study finish semantics
- `modes/free-draw.md`
  - open when: the task does not fit a more specific mode
  - owns: general free-draw finish semantics

## Observation

- `observation/visual-observation.md`
  - open when: reading or re-reading whole/part geometry, projection, depth, overlap, or identity-bearing evidence
  - owns: evidence gathering and whole ↔ relation ↔ part observation
- `observation/measuring-boundaries.md`
  - open when: bounded measurements, grids, plumb lines, or ambiguous boundaries would materially help
  - owns: measurement limits and evidence-versus-inference boundary

  - owns: gesture construction and major masses
- `construction/orientation-and-twist.md`

  - owns: major orientation, twist, and anti-flattening
- `construction/balance-and-limbs.md`

  - owns: balance and articulated limb construction
- `construction/foreshortening-and-depth.md`

  - owns: foreshortening and depth construction

## Description

- `description/descriptive-geometry.md`
  - open when: justified structure needs economical form-defining description
  - owns: descriptive geometry and geometry-versus-material distinction
- `description/contour-and-overlap.md`
  - open when: contour ownership, overlap, occlusion/reappearance, or contact is unclear
  - owns: visible contour/overlap/contact description
- `description/value-edge-and-graphite.md`
  - open when: value family, edge behavior, or graphite rendering materially affects the requested finish
  - owns: descriptive value and edge treatment

## Markmaking

Open this branch only when the geometry is justified and the remaining problem concerns mark language or material behavior.

- `markmaking/style-policy.md`
  - open when: selecting or reconciling whole-drawing material/style policy
  - owns: canonical, manga-light, and expressive-graphite style policy
- `markmaking/stroke-role-vocabulary.md`
  - open when: a mark needs a semantic role before authoring or correction
  - owns: semantic stroke roles
- `markmaking/tool-preset-selection.md`
  - open when: choosing a reusable public tool preset
  - owns: preset selection boundary
- `markmaking/stroke-dynamics.md`
  - open when: pressure, width, opacity, or authored stroke dynamics need adjustment
  - owns: stroke dynamics
- `markmaking/pressure-and-terminals.md`
  - open when: contact, release, flick, residue, or terminal span is wrong
  - owns: semantic terminal behavior
- `markmaking/broad-graphite.md`
  - open when: broad graphite core/shoulder/contact behavior is limiting
  - owns: broad graphite material behavior
- `markmaking/custom-tools.md`
  - open when: built-in presets cannot express a justified mark behavior
  - owns: bounded custom-tool escalation

## Review

- `review/visual-quality-gates.md`
  - open when: observed work targets a finished/substantially-resolved drawing and a descriptive semantic group is about to be accepted
  - owns: semantic-group evidence acceptance gates
- `review/residual-correction.md`
  - open when: a visible mismatch has been identified and a correction decision is needed
  - owns: residual description, prioritization, correction, and re-inspection logic
- `review/residual-routing.md`
  - open when: the visible symptom may not identify its causal owner
  - owns: cause-based local/upstream routing
- `review/markmaking-residuals.md`
  - open when: geometry may already be correct but the mark role, dynamics, style, or material reads wrong
  - owns: geometry-versus-markmaking residual routing
- `review/stroke-retirement.md`
  - open when: provisional/search/construction marks may have outlived their purpose
  - owns: KEEP / SOFTEN / RETIRE decisions
- `review/authored-element-navigation.md`
  - open when: existing authored elements must be found before replacement/deletion/editing
  - owns: authored-element navigation
- `review/completion.md`
  - open when: deciding whether the current drawing is artistically complete
  - owns: current-evidence finish judgment and remaining-residual/limitation decision
