# Instruction graph index

This file is a **map**, not a textbook, lifecycle, or preload curriculum. Start at `SKILL.md`, then open only the smallest leaves that own the current problem.

Paths here are relative to `references/`; `SKILL.md` uses the `references/...` prefix for the same leaves.

## Foundation

- `foundation/reference-authority.md` — open when: source authority or plausibility conflicts are in question; owns: observed/imaginative/hybrid authority and anti-normalization.
- `foundation/line-economy.md` — open when: deciding which marks are necessary or search lines accumulate; owns: economical semantic grouping without geometry loss.
- `foundation/structural-specificity.md` — open when: structure is becoming generic or downstream description will inherit it; owns: structural specificity and revalidation of provisional construction.
- `foundation/occlusion-inference.md` — open when: an important relation disappears behind another form; owns: minimum hidden continuity reasoning versus visible rendered appearance.
- `foundation/scope-and-precedence.md` — open when: requirements or correction priorities compete; owns: scope, precedence, and macro-before-micro priority.

## Drawing modes

- `modes/gesture-drawing.md` — open when: gesture is the requested mode or explicit intermediate pass; owns: pure/constructive gesture and gesture-specific completion.
- `modes/croquis.md` — open when: the requested finish is croquis; owns: croquis mark economy and finish boundary.
- `modes/figure-drawing.md` — open when: the requested finish is resolved figure drawing; owns: figure-drawing emphasis and finish expectations.
- `modes/line-study.md` — open when: the finish emphasizes line over tone; owns: line-study finish semantics.
- `modes/tonal-study.md` — open when: the finish materially depends on value/light; owns: tonal-study finish semantics.
- `modes/free-draw.md` — open when: no more specific mode fits; owns: general free-draw finish semantics.

## Observation

- `observation/visual-observation.md` — open when: reading or re-reading geometry, projection, depth, overlap, or identity evidence; owns: evidence gathering and whole ↔ relation ↔ part observation.
- `observation/measuring-boundaries.md` — open when: bounded measurements or ambiguous boundaries would help; owns: measurement limits and evidence-versus-inference boundary.

## Construction

- `construction/gesture-and-masses.md` — open when: dominant flow or occupied masses are unresolved; owns: gesture construction and major masses.
- `construction/orientation-and-twist.md` — open when: turn, near/far planes, counter-rotation, or flattening is unresolved; owns: major orientation, twist, and anti-flattening.
- `construction/balance-and-limbs.md` — open when: support, balance, limb chains, terminals, or major negative spaces are unresolved; owns: balance and articulated limb construction.
- `construction/foreshortening-and-depth.md` — open when: projection compression, near/far order, overlap, or terminal orientation is unresolved; owns: foreshortening and depth construction.

## Description

- `description/descriptive-geometry.md` — open when: justified structure needs economical form-defining description; owns: descriptive geometry and geometry-versus-material distinction.
- `description/contour-and-overlap.md` — open when: contour ownership, overlap, occlusion/reappearance, or contact is unclear; owns: visible contour/overlap/contact description.
- `description/value-edge-and-graphite.md` — open when: value family, edge behavior, or graphite affects the requested finish; owns: descriptive value and edge treatment.

## Markmaking

Open this branch only when geometry is justified and the remaining problem concerns mark language or material behavior.

- `markmaking/style-policy.md` — open when: selecting or reconciling whole-drawing material/style policy; owns: canonical, manga-light, and expressive-graphite style policy.
- `markmaking/stroke-role-vocabulary.md` — open when: a mark needs a semantic role; owns: semantic stroke roles.
- `markmaking/tool-preset-selection.md` — open when: choosing a reusable public tool preset; owns: preset selection boundary.
- `markmaking/stroke-dynamics.md` — open when: pressure, width, opacity, or stroke dynamics need adjustment; owns: stroke dynamics.
- `markmaking/pressure-and-terminals.md` — open when: contact, release, flick, residue, or terminal span is wrong; owns: semantic terminal behavior.
- `markmaking/broad-graphite.md` — open when: broad graphite core/shoulder/contact behavior is limiting; owns: broad graphite material behavior.
- `markmaking/custom-tools.md` — open when: built-in presets cannot express a justified mark; owns: bounded custom-tool escalation.

## Figure

- `figure/head-face-hair.md` — open when: head orientation, feature placement, identity, or hair mass/clumps are limiting; owns: head, face, and hair description.
- `figure/torso-arms-hands.md` — open when: torso-to-arm or upper-body relations are limiting; owns: torso/arm relationship description.
- `figure/hands-and-grip.md` — open when: hand projection, finger grouping, grip, or hand/prop contact is limiting; owns: hands and grip.
- `figure/legs-feet.md` — open when: lower-limb taper, transitions, foot orientation, footwear, stance, or grounding is limiting; owns: legs and feet.
- `figure/clothing-folds.md` — open when: folds, tension, compression, drape, or clothing structure is limiting; owns: clothing-fold description.

## Props and environment

- `props/attached-objects.md` — open when: held/attached object, strap, grip, or body-object overlap/contact is limiting; owns: attached-object geometry and body contact.
- `environment/ground-and-context.md` — open when: grounding or contextual structure is needed for the drawing to read; owns: ground/contact and contextual structure.

## Review

- `review/visual-quality-gates.md` — open when: observed finished/substantially-resolved work is about to accept a descriptive semantic group; owns: semantic-group evidence acceptance gates.
- `review/residual-correction.md` — open when: a visible mismatch needs a correction decision; owns: residual description, prioritization, correction, and re-inspection logic.
- `review/residual-routing.md` — open when: the visible symptom may not identify its causal owner; owns: cause-based local/upstream routing.
- `review/markmaking-residuals.md` — open when: geometry may be correct but role, dynamics, style, or material reads wrong; owns: geometry-versus-markmaking residual routing.
- `review/stroke-retirement.md` — open when: provisional/search/construction marks may have outlived their purpose; owns: KEEP / SOFTEN / RETIRE decisions.
- `review/authored-element-navigation.md` — open when: authored elements must be found before editing; owns: authored-element navigation.
- `review/completion.md` — open when: deciding whether the current drawing is artistically complete; owns: current-evidence finish judgment and remaining-residual/limitation decision.

## Output

- `output/render-profile-and-replay.md` — open when: producing final PNG, replay, or timelapse; owns: persisted render-profile authority and end-to-end replay/output contract.

## Public runtime API

- `api/public-surface.md` — open when: programmatic marks, edits, inspection, provenance, finish, or export must be performed; owns: supported worker-facing runtime operations.
- `api/runtime-discovery.md` — open when: runtime capabilities must be inspected without private implementation; owns: public capability discovery and report-not-bypass behavior.
