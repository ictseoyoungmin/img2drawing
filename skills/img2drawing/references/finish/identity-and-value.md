# Finish intent: pose, subject, form-light, and expressive

Finish is an authoring target, not a stage, gate, density target, or automatic verdict.
Resolve the current target with `resolve_finish_guide(intent.finish_intent)` and enact its
plain-data policy through the same `DrawingSession` used for construction and correction.

The three guide types are orthogonal:

- `ModeGuide` selects the observations and construction vocabulary useful for the kind
  of drawing;
- `FinishGuide` selects the relationships, omissions, marks, values, and edges needed for
  the declared stopping target; and
- `StyleGuide` describes how those chosen marks should behave materially.

If advice conflicts, observed geometry and explicit preserved constraints come first,
then finish intent, then style preference. No guide advances or closes the session.

## Macro gate shared by every finish

Finish cannot compensate for a wrong pose, proportion, occupied mass, balance, overlap,
contact, or major prop relationship. Inspect the whole image before local refinement.
Name what each proposed line separates, confirm that the observation method can see that
boundary, and confirm that a termination was observed instead of inferred from anatomy or
habit. For tonal work, remove tone mentally or in inspection: major form must still read.

## Pose finish

Strengthen gesture, support, balance, stance, silhouette, major masses, limb chains,
ground contact, and the major prop relation. Keep the statement economical. Dense facial
features, surface texture, and blanket contour confirmation are normally omitted.

## Identity relations

For `subject` finish, recognition is relational rather than an inventory of details:

- face: feature spacing and direction against the turned centreline, intervals, and
  cheek/jaw/hair occlusion;
- hair: outer envelope, major groups, termination direction, and overlap with the face,
  neck, and body;
- hands and feet: parent joint chain, visible mass, contact, and whether the terminal is
  visible, partial, or occluded;
- clothing: garment mass and openings, compression/tension, body overlap, and only the
  distinctive seams that carry recognition; and
- props: topology, width changes, body contact, overlap, terminal mass, and distinctive
  parts after the occupied envelope reads.

A face is not resolved merely because eyes, nose, and mouth exist. A pocketed hand is not
resolved by inventing fingers: show the observed forearm–pocket contact and stop at the
occlusion. Preserve macro pose, proportion, mass, and contact while adding identity.

## Value and edge

For `form_light` finish, correct contour, thickness, separation, garment volume, and
contact before tone. Organize large observed light/shadow families, value range, edge
hierarchy, and form turn. Author broad tone as calibrated `fill_region()` decisions and
revise a disproved region rather than stacking another fill. Tone cannot manufacture
missing geometry, and a final PNG filter is not tonal finish.

## Expressive finish

Declare the composition, focal relation, rhythm, energy, and simplification rule. Record
the geometry, reference contacts, and transformation limits that must survive. Allocate
stronger marks, contrast, and edge focus selectively; never silently sacrifice required
structure or substitute a post-filter for authored decisions.

## Repeated marks inherit variation from structure

Unless specified in the user's order, the hatch is not used.
Do not draw repeated marks—hair tips, folds, laces, or strands—as decorative
rows. Five tips with identical length, angle, width, and aligned bases read as
saw teeth rather than hair. Two widely spaced crossing hatch layers read as a
net rather than black cloth.

Each mark must inherit its length, direction, and width from the structure it
terminates. A tip is the end of its strand group; a fold is the consequence of
the joint beneath it. Before drawing repeated elements, first answer: "What are
these the ends of?"

## Selective accents

Restate only a few of the most informative lines as accents. Blanket confirmation,
unlimited micro-folds, and repeated hair strands can hide residuals rather than
finish the drawing. Check the material on the current render profile and canvas
without changing geometric truth.
