## Primary visual questions

- Does the head read as an oriented cranial/jaw volume rather than a circle with placed feature icons?
- Do eyes, nose, mouth, ears, and other identity lines follow the head turn and projected face structure?
- Is the hair outer mass solved before internal strand accents?
- Are major parting and clump boundaries limited to observed structure?
- Are selected strand accents sparse/subordinate rather than carrying the silhouette?
- Are jaw, hair, neck, collar, shoulder, and background boundaries kept under correct ownership?
- Does focal line hierarchy support the face without leaving search marks as equal competitors?

## Failure signatures to watch

- circle/sphere head surviving as the visible final shape without observed profile/jaw consequence;
- facial features placed independently of the head turn;
- many near-parallel hair strokes before mass/clumps are credible;
- strand accumulation used to hide an incorrect outer hair silhouette;
- hair line continuing into arm/collar/background by convenience;
- faint face construction remaining after final feature placement without unique information;
- focal facial marks and incidental hair/context marks competing at equal weight.
