# S07 independent material and figure review

The bounded comparison is complete. The controlled material panel was accepted without an overall material preference. The independent figure reviewer preferred the mixed-media candidate, with **qualified acceptance**; the pinned v1.3.0 graphite result was **rejected** against the common substantially resolved drawing goal. This records the actual verdict, not an unconditional artistic PASS.

## Protocol and provenance

Two fresh contexts received the same private reference, goal and declared budgets: 1024×1536, supersample 2, 1,400 actions, 2,400 seconds and 55,000 tokens. They inherited the same session model without override, used separately installed pinned wheels, and read permitted public skill/API documentation. Isolation was instruction-scoped on a shared host, not an OS sandbox. Tokens were unmetered. Initial packet reading preceded the first retained artifact timestamps slightly.

The controlled probe used exactly eight paths/328 points with the same pressure and nominal width intent. Graphite deposition and ink diameter differ semantically, so equal geometry does not imply equal appearance. New area, hole, white-paint, density and gradient operations were reviewed separately. See [material metadata](measured_material.json) and [reproducible harness](material_probe.py).

The figure drawings were independent authoring attempts, not fixed-geometry material swaps. The reviewer received randomly labeled PNGs and six same-coordinate crops selected from the reference before either output was viewed. Versions, worker reports and identity mapping were withheld; apparent media remained inferable. No source masks, tracing or image generation were used. No private images, coordinates or sessions are included here.

## Blinded figure verdict

| Dimension | Preference |
| --- | --- |

| Hair mass and clumps | Tie |
| Hand/contact and occlusion | Mixed candidate |
| Line hierarchy | Mixed candidate |
| Dark boundaries and light reserves | Mixed candidate |
| Tone organization | Mixed candidate |
| Overall fidelity and coherence | Mixed candidate |

The mixed result maintains more coherent dark clothing, equipment and boots, clearer light reserves and better cuff/wrist/stock separation. Its expression and hair remain generalized, glove articulation is schematic, and conspicuous screens and background patches compete with small detail. Qualified acceptance does not certify exact likeness.

The graphite result preserves the pose, a quiet setting and a modest local face advantage. Its pale jacket, shorts, equipment and boots remain insufficiently developed against dense stockings, producing an uneven finish. That is a result-specific value allocation and authoring weakness, not proof that graphite cannot resolve the task.

## Residual ownership and limits

- Geometry: reference-specific expression, hair clump rhythm, glove/finger contact and some garment/object overlaps remain abbreviated in both results.
- Material/value organization: repeated screens flatten some mixed surfaces; the graphite result has uneven dark-mass development and weak boot continuity.
- Uncertain: PNG inspection cannot isolate authoring, raster sampling and display scaling as causes of crowded fine patterns.

One reference and one run per material do not establish general quality, model or speed superiority. The new medium does not select geometry or solve likeness automatically. The review satisfies the required comparison and residual-accounting step; rejection of the baseline and the candidate qualifications are retained in the release decision. Runtime correctness is established separately by replay, install, CI and package evidence.
