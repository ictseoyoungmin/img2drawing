# B15 — Drawing-mode generalization

State: **SKELETON**
Depends: B14

## Goal

Prove through sequential dogfood that one core supports materially different drawing
modes. API coverage alone is not evidence of generalization.

## Dogfood matrix

| Run | Intent | Primary proof | Must not regress |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | observed · croquis · pose · pencil_loose | gesture, balance, mass, line economy | B13 macro quality |
| M2 | observed · figure_drawing · subject · graphite_academic | form/contact and identity-critical finish | pose/mass |
| M3 | observed · tonal_study · form_light · graphite_academic | shadow grouping, light, edge hierarchy | proportion/mass |
| M4 | observed · free_draw · expressive · custom | interpretation and expressive intent | declared preserved constraints |
| M5 | imaginative · free_draw · expressive · preset | intent-to-drawing correction | history/replay |
| M6 | hybrid · figure/free_draw · subject · custom | preserve + transform constraints | required reference relations |

Run one row at a time. A mode may be removed or its contract revised when dogfood does
not show unique product value.

## Deliverables

- per-run intent, guide, provenance, representative evidence, and direct review;
- cross-mode comparison of observation, grammar, omissions, residuals, finish, and cost;
- only abstractions repeated across real runs, not a prefilled 20-mode registry.

## Definition of Closed

- [ ] M1–M6 or an explicitly reduced evidence-backed matrix runs sequentially through
      the same session/history/inspection/correction core.
- [ ] Each retained mode has visible mode-specific value, not metadata-only differences.
- [ ] Tonal work explains form through large value/edge structure rather than line-only
      construction plus a filter.
- [ ] Imaginative work compares against declared intent without fake reference authority.
- [ ] Hybrid work records preserved and transformed constraints separately.
- [ ] No mode-specific session/pipeline or automatic artistic PASS appears.
- [ ] Cross-mode regressions, cost, limitations, and capsule are complete.

## Non-goals

Large style libraries and exhaustive free-draw/style failure testing remain B16.
