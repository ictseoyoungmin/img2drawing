# B09 — Mode-aware finish and recognition

State: **SKELETON**
Depends: B08

## Goal

Make drawing/finish intent change visible authoring decisions while preserving macro
pose, proportion, mass, and composition.

## Deliverables

- croquis finish guidance: selective contour, line economy, limited detail;
- figure/subject finish guidance: form, overlap/contact, hands/feet, identity-critical
  face/hair/clothing/prop relations;
- tonal finish guidance: large shadow family, value grouping, edge hierarchy, form turn;
- free-draw finish guidance based on declared expressive/composition intent;
- before/after evidence showing visible differences on controlled inputs.

## Definition of Closed

- [ ] At least two finish intents produce visibly different, directly reviewed stroke/
      value/detail decisions through the same core.
- [ ] Recognition is a quality target, not a new P7 or lifecycle gate.
- [ ] Detail does not mask or regress macro errors; preserved constraints are explicit.
- [ ] Face/hair/hand/foot/prop checks are relational and subject-specific when required.
- [ ] Tonal guidance groups light/shadow rather than adding arbitrary dark bands.
- [ ] Tests validate data/provenance only; visual acceptance remains human/Agent review.
- [ ] Evidence, regression results, and capsule are complete.

## Non-goals

Universal completion verdicts, full mode matrix, automatic likeness scoring, and new
material renderer families.
