# B10 — Intent-aware completion

State: **ACTIVE**
Depends: B09

## Goal

Define “done” as an Agent decision that no **material residual for the declared intent**
remains in the current state. Completion is not a checklist count, stage, schema fill
percentage, action threshold, or automatic artistic PASS.

## Product contract

Introduce the smallest portable finish provenance needed to bind completion to current
truth. A likely shape is:

```text
FinishRecord
  intent_digest
  drawing_state_hash
  final_inspection_id
  history_cursor
  accepted_limitations[]
  unresolved_nonmaterial_notes[]
  rationale
```

The exact field names may change during B10, but these invariants may not:

- a finish record describes an Agent decision, not an artistic certificate;
- it binds to the exact current drawing + current intent + fresh inspection;
- a material mutation after finish makes the record stale;
- stale finish cannot be reused to claim completion;
- a material residual discovered after finish returns to the ordinary B06 correction loop
  rather than “reopening a finish stage”.

## Completion questions by finish intent

### `pose`

- gesture and line of action;
- balance / support / stance;
- silhouette and major masses;
- limb-chain relation and major overlaps;
- major prop relation/contact.

### `subject`

All pose-level concerns remain material plus the identity-bearing relations selected by
B09: face/hair/hands/feet/clothing/prop. Dense detail everywhere is not required.

### `form_light`

- light and shadow families;
- value grouping/range;
- form turning;
- edge hierarchy;
- line/tone-off geometry remains structurally valid.

### `expressive`

- composition and focal hierarchy;
- rhythm / shape language;
- selective simplification;
- style consistency;
- all explicitly preserved geometry/reference constraints.

## Deliverables

- versioned finish/completion provenance record or equivalent minimal session surface;
- stale-state validation and checkpoint/resume integrity;
- accepted limitation semantics that cannot hide a material residual;
- canonical guidance for post-finish correction without stage reopen vocabulary;
- deterministic negative fixtures for premature/stale finish;
- B10 capsule and status transition to B11.

## Definition of Closed

- [ ] Finish is bound to current drawing state, current intent version, and a fresh
      inspection/evidence reference.
- [ ] A material mutation makes the previous finish state stale.
- [ ] A changed intent cannot silently reuse the prior finish decision.
- [ ] Accepted limitations are explicit prose/data and are not a bypass for a known
      material residual.
- [ ] `pose`, `subject`, `form_light`, `expressive` completion questions remain guidance,
      not mandatory phase gates.
- [ ] Runtime records the Agent decision but never emits automatic artistic PASS.
- [ ] Premature/stale/tampered completion fixtures are rejected by technical invariants.
- [ ] No fresh unseen-subject dogfood is used for closure; visual completion quality is
      deferred to D01–D05 after B18.
- [ ] Capsule/docs/tests complete and B11 is next sole WIP.

## Non-goals

Renderer parity, cross-agent reproducibility, full mode/style validation, universal
likeness scoring, and fresh dogfood.
