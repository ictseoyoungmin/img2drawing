# B10 — Intent-aware completion

State: **SKELETON**
Depends: B09

## Goal

Define “done” as absence of material residual for the declared intent, not as checklist,
phase, action-count, or schema completion.

## Deliverables

- completion questions for pose, subject, form-light, and expressive intents;
- finish record bound to current state, intent version, final inspection, and accepted
  limitations;
- reopen/correction guidance when a material residual is found after finish;
- negative fixtures for premature completion.

## Definition of Closed

- [ ] Pose completion asks about gesture, balance, stance, silhouette, and major prop.
- [ ] Subject completion asks about identity-critical relations without requiring dense
      detail everywhere.
- [ ] Form-light completion asks about light family, shadow grouping, value range, edge
      hierarchy, and form turn.
- [ ] Expressive completion asks about composition, shape rhythm, focal hierarchy, and
      style consistency against declared intent.
- [ ] A stale or materially changed state cannot reuse a finish record.
- [ ] Runtime records the decision but does not auto-PASS artistic quality.
- [ ] Premature-close cases fail direct review; tests and capsule pass.

## Non-goals

Renderer parity, cross-agent reproducibility, and broad mode/style validation.
