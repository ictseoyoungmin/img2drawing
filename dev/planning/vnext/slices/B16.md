# B16 — Style and free-draw hardening

State: **SKELETON**
Depends: B15

## Goal

Harden preset/override/custom style behavior and imaginative/hybrid free-draw failure
regimes without turning style into a post-filter or spawning pipelines.

## Deliverables

- small evidence-backed preset set; start from styles already proven in B15;
- one-base-plus-overrides resolution and human-readable custom `StyleGuide` format;
- mid-session style-change provenance with explicit keep/retire/replace/add decisions;
- imaginative/hybrid intent fixtures for composition, rhythm, focal hierarchy, shape
  language, and preserved constraints;
- failure dossier for style/reference conflicts and ambiguous custom prose.

## Definition of Closed

- [ ] Controlled comparisons show visible line character, density, value, edge, and
      simplification differences caused by authoring decisions.
- [ ] Applying a style does not erase subject geometry truth or silently mutate strokes.
- [ ] Mid-session change is replayable and does not fork authoritative history.
- [ ] Invalid overrides, unknown base, ambiguous prose, and conflicting constraints have
      explicit error/escalation behavior.
- [ ] Free-draw correction remains meaningful without overlay/registration authority.
- [ ] Preset count is justified by dogfood; no inheritance graph, plugin marketplace, or
      per-style renderer/session tree.
- [ ] Visual comparisons, replay tests, limitations, and capsule pass.

## Non-goals

Unlimited media simulation or a general-purpose style DSL.
