## Purpose

B09 connects the four existing `finish_intent` values to distinct authoring policy
without adding a finish lifecycle:

```text
DrawingIntent.finish_intent
  → resolve_finish_guide()
  → Agent selects explicit mark/value/edge decisions
  → the existing DrawingSession/history records those decisions
```

`pose`, `subject`, `form_light`, and `expressive` remain plain selections. A
`FinishGuide` is immutable data containing priorities, preserved constraints,
mark/value/edge policies, omissions, relational observations, and completion questions.
It has no stage, cursor, close/reopen operation, score, or artistic verdict.

## Public surface

```python
from img2drawing import DrawingIntent, DrawingSession, resolve_finish_guide

intent = DrawingIntent(finish_intent="subject")
session = DrawingSession.create(subject="subject.png", output_dir="out", intent=intent)
finish = resolve_finish_guide(intent.finish_intent)
```

`FinishGuide` and `FinishRelation` round-trip through strict versioned plain-data schemas.
Unknown and lifecycle-bearing fields are rejected. Resolving a guide does not mutate
session geometry or create a second history.

## Durable authoring contract

- Macro pose, proportion, mass, balance, overlap/contact, and prop relation cannot be
  hidden by detail, tone, or style.
- Subject recognition is relational across face, hair, hands/feet, clothing, and props;
  detail inventory is not evidence of recognition.
- A partially occluded terminal is stopped at the observed contact. Hidden fingers,
  hair endings, anatomy, or prop parts are not invented.
- Form-light requires line/tone-off structural readability and uses compact calibrated
  value-region decisions instead of arbitrary bands or authored micro-hatch expansion.
- Expressive simplification records the geometry/reference constraints it preserves.
- `ModeGuide` owns observation/construction vocabulary, `FinishGuide` owns the stopping
  target, and `StyleGuide` owns material mark behavior. Geometry truth outranks finish,
  which outranks conflicting style preference.
