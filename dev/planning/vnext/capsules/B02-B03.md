## Contract delivered

`img2drawing.inspection` is the single stage-free inspection path. An Agent supplies
subject and current drawing images, a `Registration`, zero or more explicit ROIs,
guides, and optional read-only measurements. `InspectionSheet.create(...)` requires
the current `drawing_state_hash`, or derives the stage-free digest from a supplied
`StrokeIR`; `write(...)` emits one whole/ROI sheet plus raw drawing, registered
drawing, contrast overlay, and portable JSON artifacts.

The registration is explicit and axis-aligned:

```text
canvas = offset + subject * scale
```

ROI boxes and grid/guides use subject coordinates. The registered drawing is a
presentation mapping only; it never rewrites authored geometry. Measurement helpers
provide mapping, point, distance, angle, grid, profiles, pixel samples, plumb, and
ground-guide data. They make no semantic judgement and do not create a verdict.

## Frozen invariants

- `drawing_state_hash` removes only top-level `metadata.history_cursor` and each
  serialized stroke's `stage` while retaining authored geometry/material state,
  including nested authored keys. It is separate from the legacy R23 `state_hash`
  and is the identity bound by this slice.
- `subject_sha256`, `drawing_artifact_sha256`, and `drawing_state_hash` are all
  recorded. The first identifies the subject bytes, the second identifies the
  exact raw drawing artifact copied into the sheet, and the third identifies the
  authored stage-free logical drawing state.
- Raw drawing and altered contrast overlay are separate artifacts. Ink over dark
  subject regions uses a strong cyan channel; other ink uses red.
- Manifests contain input basenames and output-relative artifact names only; the
  three SHA-256 bindings contain no machine-local paths.
- The package has one inspection implementation and does not alter `run.py`, core
  session/history, stages, review, checkpoint, replay, or renderer code.
- `PASS`/`FAIL`, anatomy judgement, automatic registration, ROI selection, and stroke
  mutation are outside the inspection foundation.

## Reopen resolution

- R1/R2: PIL output-to-input raster mapping now uses
  `(scale_x, 0, offset_x, 0, scale_y, offset_y)`, with a non-identity pixel
  correspondence regression.
- R3: subject bytes, copied raw drawing bytes, and stage-free logical state are
  independently hash-bound in both inspection manifests.
- R4: stage-free normalization is structural rather than recursive over arbitrary
  authored metadata/tool state.
- R5/R6: the representative sheet, raw/registered/contrast rasters, profiles,
  measurements, and direct visual review are committed and inspectable.

## B04 handoff

B04 may consume `InspectionSheet` through a thin `inspect(...)` facade, but must not
create a second inspection path. It should pass the stage-free drawing digest from
the session adapter and preserve the subject-space registration contract. The
inspection module remains read-only and does not decide when a session is complete.

## Reopen conditions

Reopen B02+B03 only if a representative current drawing cannot be inspected from one
sheet, dark-region ink becomes unreadable, input paths leak into artifacts, the sheet
binds to the legacy stage-sensitive digest, or a future change introduces semantic
judgement or geometry mutation into the measurement path.
