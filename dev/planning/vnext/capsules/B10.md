## Purpose

B10 replaces arbitrary vNext `finish(metadata)` claims with one portable record bound to
current truth:

```text
current DrawingIntent
+ current drawing-state hash/history cursor
+ latest inspection made under that intent
+ no open material ResidualRecord
+ Agent rationale/limitations
→ FinishRecord
```

This is decision provenance, not an artistic certificate. It creates no stage, cursor,
score, PASS/FAIL, close/reopen transition, or edit lock.

## Public surface

```python
record = session.finish(
    final_inspection_id=session.inspection_history[-1]["inspection_id"],
    rationale="No material pose residual remains in the current inspection.",
    accepted_limitations=("facial detail omitted for pose finish",),
    unresolved_nonmaterial_notes=(),
)
assert session.finish_record == record
assert session.finish_is_current
```

`FinishRecord` is strict immutable data with a versioned schema. The session derives its
intent digest, drawing-state hash, and history cursor. Inspection records now carry the
cursor and intent digest observed at capture. Resume cross-validates these fields against
the action history, intent provenance, immutable inspection, and finish record.

## Durable completion contract

- Finish requires a declared intent and the latest inspection of the current state under
  that intent.
- Every open `ResidualRecord` is material and prevents finish. Accepted limitations and
  nonmaterial notes cannot bypass it.
- A later authored mutation, intent change, or newly opened material residual preserves
  the record but makes `finish_is_current` false.
- Continued work uses the ordinary inspect/residual/correct loop; no finish stage is
  reopened.
- A new current inspection may support a replacement finish record. Reusing stale or old
  intent evidence is rejected.
- Pre-B10 `finish_metadata` resumes only as noncanonical historical metadata and never
  makes the session currently finished.

## Consolidation

The B09 and B10 verifier entry points now share `dev/tools/vnext_verification.py` for CLI,
environment, and pytest execution. Each slice keeps only its contract-specific assertions.
No second completion history, workflow, or legacy identity-finish manifest was added.

## Evidence and checks

- Deterministic fixture: `dev/fixtures/vnext-b10/run.py`.
- Focused completion/session/intent regression: 30 passed.
- Full development regression: 161 passed, 5 pre-existing JSON-schema deprecation
  warnings.
- Negative coverage: premature finish, stale drawing inspection, changed intent,
  unresolved material residual, correlated checkpoint tampering, rollback failure, and
  noncanonical legacy metadata.
- Direct API review found no automatic verdict, finish lifecycle, or session lock.

The fixture uses synthetic input and proves mechanics only. General visual completion,
unseen-subject robustness, and cross-agent consistency remain deferred until D01-D06.

## Reopen conditions

Reopen B10 if a completion claim can detach from its intent/state/inspection, survive a
material change as current, hide an open residual, accept correlated source tampering, or
turn into an artistic verdict or workflow gate.
