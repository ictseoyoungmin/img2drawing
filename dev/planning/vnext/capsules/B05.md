## Handoff summary

B05 adds the first whole-figure construction surface over the stage-free B04
`DrawingSession`. The worker records a short `PoseObservation`, authors explicit
subject-space marks, commits them atomically, and inspects the result immediately.

The conceptual vocabulary is:

`read pose → line of action → head/ribcage/pelvis mass → balance/plumb → joints/limbs`

The names are drawing vocabulary only, not runtime state, stages, manifests,
advancement gates, or a required tuple order. `InitialConstruct` validates phase
membership and geometry while preserving agent-authored mark order.

## Public surface

Use:

```text
PoseObservation + ConstructionMark* → InitialConstruct
InitialConstruct → author_initial_construct(session)
session → inspect_initial_construct(session, construct)
```

`DrawingSession` remains the only history, checkpoint, renderer, and inspection
authority. B05 does not infer pose or create a parallel drawing path.

## Dogfood truth boundary

The canonical runner is `dev/dogfood/vnext-b05/run.py`. It receives only the
subject image and drawing mode/skill context. It authors a subject-specific x/y/z
hypothesis for the back/side turn, near/right arm occlusion, far/left elbow
projection, turned masses, balance, legs/feet, and prop. The Imagen
`ideal-stroke-reference.png` is a capability exemplar only and is not loaded.

Representative evidence is under `dev/evidence/vnext/b05/`; `REVIEW.md` records
the direct visual comparison and independent native subagent audit. All macro
criteria passed, including non-frontal pose, arm occlusion, elbow projection,
weight shift, knee/ankle direction, non-rail stance, and absence of joint circles.

The reopen changed only the near/right arm center/outer/inner authored paths and its
two depth contours. The final projected arm paths turn the forearm outward before the
wrist/hand returns toward the waist; all other accepted body geometry stayed frozen.
