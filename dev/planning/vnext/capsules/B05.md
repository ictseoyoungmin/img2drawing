## Handoff summary

B05 adds the first whole-figure construction surface over the stage-free B04
`DrawingSession`. The worker records a short `PoseObservation`, authors explicit
subject-space marks, commits them atomically, and inspects the result immediately.

The conceptual vocabulary is:

`read pose → line of action → head/ribcage/pelvis mass → balance/plumb → joints/limbs`

The names are drawing vocabulary only, not runtime state, stages, manifests,
advancement gates, or a required tuple order. `InitialConstruct` validates phase
membership and geometry while preserving agent-authored mark order.

## Public surface

Use:

```text
PoseObservation + ConstructionMark* → InitialConstruct
InitialConstruct → author_initial_construct(session)
session → inspect_initial_construct(session, construct)
```

`DrawingSession` remains the only history, checkpoint, renderer, and inspection
authority. B05 does not infer pose or create a parallel drawing path.
